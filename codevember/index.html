Fold

Study

A flat grid grows, folds seven times and is cut in two; the halves open like the pages of a book to show their cut faces. Soft candy gum in pastel layers, and a shadow that falls only on the floor.

Tension

Study

A rope stretched top to bottom, about to snap: five of its six strands have broken and the last one, untwisted into the middle, holds across the gap. The view zooms in and finds the same rope again, a third the size — and so on, forever.

  • rope

Study

A sea floor to draw kelp on: every stipe and blade is a chain of joints pulled by buoyancy and the current, the pointer's wake included. The wells on the left pick the kind.

Bolt

Study

A diorama of a thunderstorm: on a block of meadow, its soil layers showing over a wooden base, a bolt of lightning strikes and is held at the instant it touches the ground. Only the bolt is still; the clouds roll and the rain keeps falling.

Set

Codevember 2026 — Twenty-Three Days

Set

curllmooha's deployed set: twenty-three of the thirty WebGPU prompts, each a scene with its own live controls, textures, models and sound — a sheet folding forever, a launcher cracking a volume, three scoops melting, wool tangling, a gravity-well orrery, a hollow Earth, a kelp forest, a maple lantern, a lightning strike, a cave of echoes and a magma flow. Recovered from the public Vite bundle into an editable multi-page project; days 24–30 were not published.
