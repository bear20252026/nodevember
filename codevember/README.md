# Codevember archive

Sixteen Codevember 2026 works moved from [paper-widgets](https://github.com/bear20252026/paper-widgets) to Nodevember on 2026-11-24.

- **Three complete sets:** mrdoob's seventeen days, Yuri Artiukh's eighteen days and curllmooha's twenty-three days.
- **Thirteen studies:** twelve single-prompt WebGL studies plus loktar00's *Stepping Stones*.

The archive page is [index.html](index.html). Each folder keeps its own README, credit, source and licence. Unless a piece says otherwise, no licence is granted beyond reading and learning; the Codevember works are not relicensed by the Nodevember repository's MIT licence.

## Layout

```text
codevember/
  index.html       archive page
  catalog.json     archive metadata
  codevember-*/    the sixteen works, moved intact
```
