# Models

`*.bin`: a mesh, welded, centered and fitted in the unit sphere, its faces
counterclockwise from outside. Two `uint32` (points, triangles), the points as
`float32` xyz, the triangles as `uint16` (`uint32` from 65536 points on).

- `lee-perry-smith.bin` — the head scan by Lee Perry-Smith (Infinite Realities),
  CC BY 3.0, from three.js's `examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/LeePerrySmith.glb`.
- `bunny.bin` — the Stanford Bunny (Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory), from
  three.js's `examples/models/draco/bunny.drc`. Open at the bottom: the shell
  is walled and framed there as at the seams.
