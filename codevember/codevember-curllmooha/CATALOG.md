### 页面描述

- **01 Fold** — A sheet of paper folding in half forever, and never getting smaller.
- **02 Shatter** — Pull back a launcher. The first hit cracks it, the second brings it down, and you can rewind it all.
- **03 Melt** — Three scoops in a waffle cone, slowly melting.
- **04 Tangle** — Three balls of wool in one knot, on a blanket under a lamp at night. Pull the threads, throw the balls, snap the blanket, and wind it all back up.
- **05 Moss** — A soft moss cushion with glass marbles resting in it.
- **06 Orrery** — A gravity-well orrery: marble planets orbit an engraved brass funnel, spiral into the sun and are lifted back by an Archimedes screw.
- **07 Hollow** — An Earth in space with one glowing opening at its pole: fly in to a molten world inside, where a red-hot core drips lava onto the ground.
- **08 Tesseract** — A sapphire crystal that opens onto a repeating tunnel of blue light.
- **09 Membrane** — Stretch a translucent membrane and watch air bubbles drift through the gel.
- **10 Caustic** — Drip acid on a sculpture in silver, gold, plastic or marble and watch it fizz, stain and etch the surface.
- **11 Rust** — Watch a wooden chest weather as rust spreads across its iron fittings.
- **12 Feather** — A round creature thatched with thousands of feathers. Click it and it puffs itself up; press and hold to blow air through its coat.
- **13 Labyrinth** — A colony of ants loose in a sand labyrinth. Nobody plans the route, but their scent trails find the shortest way to the sugar. Knock walls down and watch them reroute.
- **14 Tension** — Orb webs strung across the morning, glinting with dew. Flies, leaves and raindrops blow in, the silk stretches under them, and the spiders run to wrap what they caught.
- **15 Mirage** — On foot in a dune sea at noon. Ahead, the sand turns to water that holds the sky; walk toward it and it stays exactly as far away.
- **16 Spore** — An interactive macro study of one mushroom, seen from beneath its cap.
- **17 Kelp** — A giant kelp forest seen from the reef, swaying in the swell under Snell's window.
- **18 Pendulum** — Perfect-fit pendulums: every part meets its gap on time, forever.
- **19 Erosion** — A block of land worn by a hundred thousand raindrops, live.
- **20 Cocoon** — An articulated maple lantern casting moving shadows.
- **21 Bolt** — Fly through the inside of a thunderstorm while lightning fires around you; click to call a strike.
- **22 Echo** — A pitch-black cave that appears only where the echo of a click passes. Click to send a click.
- **23 Magma** — A glowing lump of lava with a cracked black crust, smoking in the dark. Drag to orbit, click to crack it open.
